## Ongoing modelling and control work

This research develops the foundations for modelling and controlling a **Voliro-like variable-tilt aerial robot model**. We did not design or build a physical robot; the work is based on a mathematical and simulation model used to study the vehicle's dynamics, actuation, and control authority.

The work is being carried out by **Farooq Olanrewaju, Ziad Shoeib, Ahmed AbdurRazaq, MD Tarique Hamid, and Benyahia Ayman**, under the advisement of **Dr. Ramy Rashad and Dr. Sami El-Ferik**.

## Research focus

- Geometric modelling of a variable-tilt aerial robot on SE(3).
- Analysis of how tiltable rotors affect force and moment generation.
- Estimation and control strategies for the modelled vehicle.
- Control allocation for distributing desired wrenches across the actuators.
- Simulation-based evaluation of tracking and maneuverability.

## Scope

The contribution is currently centred on mathematical modelling, simulation, and controller development. Claims about a manufactured prototype, physical flight testing, or completed hardware design do not describe this research and have been removed from the theme overview.
