## MSc project portfolio

This theme brings together the marine-robotics work completed during my Erasmus Mundus Master’s degree in Marine and Maritime Intelligent Robotics, together with subsequent ship-system identification work. It spans three universities and combines modelling, autonomous underwater vehicle control, oceanographic analysis, underwater perception, docking, cooperative manipulation, and maritime computer vision.

The projects were completed across **Université de Toulon (UTLN), France; Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), Portugal; and Universitat Jaume I (UJI), Spain**. Together, they trace a progression from understanding marine environments and modelling underwater vehicles to implementing autonomy and grasp planning on robotic platforms.

## Project areas

- Autonomous underwater vehicle modelling and simulation
- Oceanographic data analysis for the Mediterranean Sea
- BlueROV trajectory planning, control, docking, and search tasks
- Vision-based perception for underwater interaction
- Grasp planning for cooperative underwater robots
- Automated ship identification and classification from satellite imagery
- Optimization-driven system identification of ship maneuvering dynamics
