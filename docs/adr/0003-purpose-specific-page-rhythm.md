# ADR-0003: Use purpose-specific page rhythm for academic reading

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The first visual rewrite applied the same large editorial hero treatment to the homepage, indexes, research records, publications, About, and Contact. Screenshot review across all routes showed that this created unnecessary whitespace, repeated oversized headings, duplicated research titles, and made scholarly evidence less prominent than presentation.

## Decision

The site will use three presentation families: compact index pages for comparison and navigation, reading pages for sustained research content, and profile/action pages for biography or contact. Research detail titles appear once at a restrained scale; missing imagery and empty future sections are omitted rather than represented by placeholders.

## Consequences

- Academic content becomes faster to scan and easier to read on small screens.
- The homepage and index routes become shorter without removing canonical content.
- Research detail pages can spend space on methods, contributions, applications, and evidence.
- Layout decisions must be evaluated against the page's content purpose instead of a single global hero pattern.
